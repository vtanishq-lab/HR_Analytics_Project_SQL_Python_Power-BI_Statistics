# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard | Data Analytics Project

## 📌 Project Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard is a data analytics project designed to
analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, job roles, and
key HR performance indicators.

The objective is to transform HR data into meaningful insights that
help organizations understand employee turnover, identify workforce
patterns, and support data-driven HR decisions.

This project combines data analysis, visualization, and statistical
interpretation to explore workforce trends and employee attrition.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall employee attrition and attrition rate.
- Identify job roles with the highest attrition count.
- Understand employee distribution across departments.
- Analyze attrition by gender and marital status.
- Explore employee demographics by age group.
- Compare attrition rates across education fields.
- Analyze attrition patterns across different job levels.
- Identify workforce trends to support employee retention.
- Present key findings through an interactive dashboard.

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Research & Development has the highest employee count.
- Sales Executives have the highest attrition count among
  the displayed job roles.
- Employees aged 26–35 represent the largest age group.
- Human Resources has the highest attrition rate among
  the displayed education fields.
- Job Level 1 has the highest attrition rate in the
  job-level analysis.
- Attrition patterns vary across gender, marital status,
  departments, job roles, and education fields.
- Workforce segmentation helps identify areas that may
  require further investigation.
